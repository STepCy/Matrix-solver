🛠️ Engineering Applications
Matrix computations form the backbone of modern engineering:
  • Cramer's Rule: Solving electrical circuit mesh currents & truss analysis.
  • LU Decomposition: Used in structural simulation and fluid dynamics solvers.
  • Eigenvalues: Crucial for vibration analysis and mechanical resonance design.
  • RREF: Finding linear independence in system control theory.
